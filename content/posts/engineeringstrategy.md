If you are some kind of leader in software development you have probably followed and/or been expected to produce an engineering strategy. I have written strategies, I have read many strategies but I come back to the question about what an engineering strategy really is. And the best definition I have found is Will Larson's [Writing an engineering strategy](https://lethain.com/eng-strategies/)

He uses Richard Rumelt’s framework. 

* Diagnosis: a theory describing the nature of the challenge. 
* Guiding policies: the approaches you’ll apply to grapple with the challenge. 
* Coherent actions: a set of specific actions directed by guiding policy to address challenge.

Go read the [article](https://lethain.com/eng-strategies/) to get all you need, examples, how and when to write it, how to relate to other company strategies and more.

date: 2025-06-08

"If we only follow the process we can solve any problem!"

You might have a great project process but the projects start to do the wrong things. And since the culture says "trust the process" people don't dare to raise their voice. Everyone tries to do the best of what they have been told to do but the end result is not what was needed.

I think the problem is that process does not take ownership, process does not care. Yes you need standardized ways of doing things (process). And in some types of activities it works great year in and year out. But in e.g. software development it is not enough. The industry change, the product changes, the customer changes. The process does not care. Therefore you need people that take ownership of outcomes. And if the process does not give the wanted outcome those people change the process.

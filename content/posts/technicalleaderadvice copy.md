Getting everyone to agree, how to handle many people with different agendas when an important decision needs to be taken. The advice was to look for the person(s) that make the final decision. There will (at least in bigger companies) be many people that want to own the decision and might think they do. But most of the time it is only one or a few people that actually own it. Look for them and work with them. Don't spend (too much) time on the others.

This might seem obvious, convince the decision makers, but I realize that in larger organisations it many times is easy to forget that not all people that want to decide things actually will be there for the final decision.

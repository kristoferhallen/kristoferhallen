Me and my colleague Conny Wickström presented at [The Future of Software conference](https://www.thefutureofsoftware.com). You can watch our talk Efficiency unleashed: The Ericsson playbook for embedded system development [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MvGGoBKYRQ).

There we many good speakers at the conference, my short summary:

* The "we have a physical product" companies need to take more space. Automotive, defense, telecom, medical... Me and my colleague wanted to show our use cases. E.g. a lot of expensive test equipment and not owning your deployment.
* Platform engineering is not about tools, it is way of working and mindset. (Of course you need tools to give you the right capabilities but wools without the right approach will not succeed)
* AI value in software development is not (so much) about creating code. It will help you be really good at all the things you need to do with the code. Review, refactor, ideation, design...

I had a talk at [EMEASEC 2026](https://events.incose.org/event/emeasec26/) in Linköping, Sweden together with [Johanna Wallén Axehill
](https://www.linkedin.com/in/johanna-axehill-61584514a/). The third author [Erik Herzog](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erik-herzog-9b597a2/) was in the audience and supported. We presented the paper Change: A Structured Method for the Technical Leader and his/her Organisation. In short - as a technical leader you need to handle change, for you as an individual, the organisation and the projects you run. We described two models that you can use to in a structured way reflect on your situation and identify areas where you need to change.

You find the paper and models we presented [here](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/413765216_Change_A_Structured_Method_for_the_Technical_Leader_and_hisher_Organisation).

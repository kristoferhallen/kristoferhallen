I have written about being [proud of the solutions you have]({{< relref "chestertonsfence.md" >}}). But how do you know when it is time to change and start using a new tool or technology? 

I end up in a "10 times rule". If changing means that you get at least 10 times better. Better could be cost, user experience, speed or something else. 

* Why not just change all the time? Change is almost always hard. You have dependencies to other tools or technologies, developer or users need to learn and change. So when you change make sure it is worth it.

* Why not go for 2 times better? Twice as fast is really good! Of course, you will many times have reasons to change even if you don't get 10 times better. But it is good to have a rule that quickly dismisses changes that give too little value, don't spend time on it. And proposals that pass the first test will be worth the time to discuss.

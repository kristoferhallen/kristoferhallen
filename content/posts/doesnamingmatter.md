In software development we run Proof of Concepts (PoCs), do Minimum Viable Products (MVPs), use DevOps and many other activities that have names that are more or less well defined. So when I want to get something done, does it matter what I call it? Yes! Or a longer answer, Yes, it matters in the context where other people interact with your activity, people that finance it, use the output or might fight against it because they don't understand what you are doing. 

E.g. a PoC is according to most definitions very far from an product that anyone can or should use. If you run an activity called PoC and then build something you expect people to use you:  
* have probably not built something that will work well with users when it comes to quality, usability.
* confuse you users, even if you have built a product they don't expect it to be a product
* confuse your stakeholders, what did they actually finance, what have you spent your money on?
´
Instead use the industry standard meaning of a term, if there is none write down what your definition is.

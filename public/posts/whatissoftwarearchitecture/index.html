What is (software) architecture

15 Dec 2024

What is software architecture? And is it needed?

I met Charity Majors at a conference recently and talked to her about her statement “architect is a bullshit role”. I am one of those that she refers to when she says “One of my favorite things in the world is when software architects come up to me and tell me how much they enjoyed my piece about how “software architect” is such a bullshit role at most companies.”

Now and then I reflect on the role of the software architect and software architecture. And then I tend to re-read Martin Fowlers Software Architecture Guide. “Architecture is about the important stuff. Whatever that is. On first blush, that sounds trite, but I find it carries a lot of richness. It means that the heart of thinking architecturally about software is to decide what is important, (i.e. what is architectural), and then expend energy on keeping those architectural elements in good condition.".

Combine that with Charity’s thinking about the need for good observability and you have a good combination - thinking about what is important and observing the system in production to improve the thinking in the next iteration.
